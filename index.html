Welcome To Jasvi Industries

We "Jasvi Industries" are a leading Manufacturer, supplier and exporter of a wide range of High-Pressure Needle Valves, 2, 3 & 5-Way Manifold Blocks, Process Ball Valves, Check Valves, Double Block & Bleed (DBB) Valves etc. Situated in Surat & Ahmedabad (Gujarat, India), We have constructed a wide and well functional infrastructural unit that plays an important role in the growth of our company. We offer these precision severe-service products at reasonable rates and deliver these within the promised time-frame.

With the consistent innovations and precision engineering in fluid handling technology, everyone is resorting to severe-service valves for bringing efficiency that too at zero leakage. We have been consistently innovating and manufacturing our products with the help of the latest 5-axis CNC technology and have been successful in providing products and services which make industrial flow operations safer and much more dependable. This fact has made us a major manufacturer and supplier of severe-service instrumentation valves. Due to these reasons, we have extended domestic boundaries across India to international export markets spanning the Middle East, Southeast Asia, Africa, and Europe. The company is growing very rapidly and has plans to extend its operations in many more countries across the globe.

We have a huge number of talented engineers and CNC tooling specialists who are qualified and skillful because of their rich experience in this industry. These specialists work very efficiently in order to manufacture the best quality valves across the industry and provide quality service to the clients.
