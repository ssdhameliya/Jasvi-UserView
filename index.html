100% INDIVIDUAL PRESSURE TESTING ASSURANCE

Every valve manufactured at our Ahmedabad facility undergoes hydrostatic shell, high-pressure seat, and low-pressure pneumatic air testing conforming strictly to API 598.

01

100% API 598 Hydrostatic Shell Test

Body shell subjected to 1.5x maximum allowable working pressure with zero structural deformation and zero perceptible leakage across holding times exceeding API/BS requirements.

02

High-Pressure Seat Tightness

Tested at 1.1x design pressure for bidirectional shut-off. Resilient elastomeric and metal-to-metal seats verified for Rate A zero-bubble drop tightness.

03

Positive Material Identification (PMI)

Handheld XRF spectroscopic alloy verification on every cast heat and forged billet lot, guaranteeing authentic ASTM A216 WCB, CF8M, and Duplex metallurgy.

04

Fire Safe API 607 / 6FA Compliance

Qualified 30-minute burn testing at 750°C-1000°C followed by forced water quench, verifying secondary graphite seal integrity during catastrophic plant fires.

05

Fugitive Emissions ISO 15848-1

Helium mass spectrometer sniffer detection across stem gland seals, guaranteeing volatile organic compound (VOC) containment to Class AH/BH standards.

06

Third-Party Inspection Agencies

Factory witness testing routinely conducted with TUV, DNV, Bureau Veritas, Lloyd's Register, Engineers India Limited (EIL), and PDIL inspection teams.
