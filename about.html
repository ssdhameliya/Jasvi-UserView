About DS Industries

Precision Flow Control Engineering From Ahmedabad, India

Guided by our core ethos — "Committed With Precision & Performance" — DS Industries is a premier manufacturer and solution provider of industrial valves, non-return check valves, slurry knife gate valves, and pipeline filtration systems. From our manufacturing and testing facility in Nikol - Naroda, Ahmedabad, we engineer flow-control equipment built to withstand high temperatures, corrosive chemicals, and extreme pressures up to ASME Class 2500#.

Manufacturing & Quality Assurance Pillars

01

Precision CNC & Mirror Lapping

Wedges, plugs, and seats are CNC-machined and lapped to a mirror finish with CoCr (Stellite) hard-facing to prevent galling and seat drag.

02

Certified ASTM Metallurgy

Full material traceability across WCB, CF8, CF8M, Forged A105/LF2, F316L, Duplex F51/F53, Monel K400, and Inconel 625.

03

Fire-Safe & Fugitive Seal

Side-entry ball valves and high-performance butterfly valves engineered to API 607 / API 6FA fire-safe standards with live-loaded V-packing.

04

100% API 598 Verification

Every valve undergoes rigorous shell, backseat, and low/high-pressure closure testing per API 598 and EN 12266-1 prior to dispatch.
